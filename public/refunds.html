Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Our Guarantee

At Affilore, we want you to feel completely confident in your purchase. That is why we offer a comprehensive 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you are not completely satisfied with our affiliate marketing micro-tools for any reason whatsoever, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date — no questions asked and no hard feelings.

2. How to Request a Refund

Getting a refund is fast, straightforward, and transparent. Follow these simple steps:

  1. 1
    Send an Email Send an email to affilore4@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request".
  2. 2
    Provide Purchase Details Include your purchase email address and order/transaction ID (which can be found in your Paddle receipt and order confirmation email).
  3. 3
    Verification and Processing Our team will review and verify your purchase information and process the refund within 3–5 business days.
  4. 4
    Credit to Payment Method The full amount will be credited back directly to your original payment method via Paddle.

3. Refund Eligibility

To keep our guarantee fair for all users, the following eligibility criteria apply:

  • 30-Day Window: Refund requests must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • One Refund per Product: Each customer is eligible for one refund per product. Subsequent purchases of the same product or plan are not eligible for refunds.
  • 100% Full Refund: Approved refunds are processed for the full purchase amount paid at checkout.
  • Finality: After the 30-day period has elapsed, all purchases are considered final and non-refundable.

4. Cancellation

Affilore operates on a straightforward one-time pricing model with lifetime or fixed-term licenses. There are no recurring monthly or annual subscription fees, which means you will never be automatically rebilled, and there is nothing to cancel.

If an ongoing subscription model is introduced for optional premium features or services in the future, explicit cancellation terms and self-service cancellation controls will be updated directly in this policy.

5. Chargebacks

We pride ourselves on responsive, friendly customer support and are dedicated to making every customer experience positive. We strongly encourage customers to contact us directly at affilore4@gmail.com before initiating a dispute or chargeback with their bank, credit card company, or PayPal.

Filing a chargeback without first contacting our support team to request a refund creates unnecessary delays, incurs merchant penalties, and may result in the immediate and permanent suspension of your Affilore account and associated licenses.

We are 100% committed to resolving any issues or dissatisfaction quickly, fairly, and professionally.

6. Paddle as Merchant of Record

All payments, billing transactions, invoicing, and refunds for Affilore are securely handled by Paddle.com Market Ltd ("Paddle"), which serves as the Merchant of Record. Paddle is responsible for customer service inquiries regarding billing, sales tax calculation and remittance (including VAT and GST), and issuing approved refunds.

Once Affilore approves your refund, Paddle will initiate the transfer back to your original payment method. Depending on your financial institution or credit card provider, it typically takes between 3 to 10 business days for the credit to appear on your bank statement.
